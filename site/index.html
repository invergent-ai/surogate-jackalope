Powered by Surogate

A high-performance training engine with a native C++/CUDA core, built for fast experimentation on-premise or in the cloud.

  • Full fine-tuning, LoRA, and FP8 / NVFP4 QLoRA
  • BF16, native FP8, and NVFP4 4-bit precision (Blackwell: B200/B300, RTX 50xx)
  • GRPO reinforcement learning with deterministic environments
  • Native multi-GPU and multi-node training, smart CPU offloading, MoE
