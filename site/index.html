One command

Zero to training in one line

Paste it into your terminal. It installs the toolchain, walks you through a quick wizard, sets up the Surogate engine, and wires the cloud clients.

$ curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/invergent-ai/surogate-jackalope/main/install.sh | sh irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/invergent-ai/surogate-jackalope/main/install.ps1 | iex
Everything in one window

From dataset to trained model

No YAML wrangling, no CLI flags, no squinting at logs over SSH.

Live Monitor

Real-time loss curve, per-GPU temp, power, util and memory, progress, and a streaming log.

One-click Launch

SFT, GRPO and RULER. Pick model, dataset and precision recipe, click your GPUs, go.

Train anywhere

Local GPUs, a remote SSH box, a Modal sandbox, or your own cloud via dstack.

Hub search

Find models and datasets on Hugging Face right inside the app and line up a run.

Runs & artifacts

Browse past runs, stream logs, and pull trained checkpoints back to disk.

Polished & native

Installs natively and runs offline. Light or dark. A few-MB binary.
